# Privacy

This page describes what the cloudground.it website does, based on its source code. [The
repository](https://github.com/cloudground-it/cloudground-docs) is public, so you can check.

## What the site collects {#what-it-collects}

Nothing. The site is a set of static pages:

- **no cookies** and no visitor analytics;
- **no forms**: if you have a question or hit a problem, open an [issue on
  GitHub](https://github.com/cloudground-it/cloudground/issues);
- **no third-party services**: fonts, search and images are all served by the site itself. Search
  runs in your browser against an index downloaded with the pages, so your search terms never
  leave your device;
- the "Was this page useful?" question at the bottom of the docs only changes what the page
  displays. Your answer is not stored or sent anywhere.

## What stays in your browser {#what-stays-in-your-browser}

If you pick the light or dark theme with the toggle in the navigation bar, your browser saves that
choice under the `theme` key in its local storage (*localStorage*). It stays on your device and is
never sent to the site. You can clear it by deleting the site's data in your browser settings. If
you never touch the toggle, the site follows your system setting and stores nothing.

## Hosting {#hosting}

The pages are served by GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub
receives the IP address and request headers of every visitor, and handles them under its [privacy
statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
This site has no access to that data.

Links to GitHub (the source code, releases and issues) take you off this site. Once you are there,
GitHub's policies apply.
