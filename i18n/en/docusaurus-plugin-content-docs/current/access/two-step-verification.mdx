With two-step verification on, the panel asks for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app after
your password. The panel has root access to the server, so turn this on for every administrator
account.

## Turn on two-step verification {#turn-it-on}

1. Open **Security** from your account menu.
2. In the **Two-step verification** section, enter your password in **Confirm with your password**.
3. Press **Set up 2FA**.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
5. Enter the **6-digit code** shown in the app and press **Verify and enable**.

If the code doesn't match, check that your phone's clock is correct. Once verification is on, the
panel signs out your other sessions. The session you used to turn it on stays open.

## Sign in with a code {#sign-in}

After your email and password, the panel asks for the **6-digit code**. Each code works only once. If
you've just used one, wait for the next. After 10 wrong codes within an hour, the panel stops checking
codes for that account until the hour is up. See
[two-step codes](/docs/explanation/sign-in-protection#two-step-codes).

## Switch to a new authenticator app {#replace-the-app}

To move to a different phone:

1. In **Security**, enter your password in **Confirm with your password** and a **Current code** from
   the old app.
2. Press **Replace the authenticator app** and scan the new QR code.
3. Confirm with a code from the new app and press **Verify and enable**.

The old app keeps working until you confirm. Starting a replacement never turns verification off.

## Turn off two-step verification {#turn-it-off}

1. In **Security**, enter your password in **To turn it off, enter your password** and a
   **Current code**.
2. Press **Disable 2FA** and confirm.

Turning off verification signs out your other sessions and **revokes your API tokens**.

## Require it for all administrators {#require-it}

An administrator can make two-step verification mandatory:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. In the **Access** section, turn on **Require 2FA for administrators**.
3. Press **Save**.

From then on, an administrator without two-step verification can only see their own **Security**
page until they set it up. Nobody gets locked out. The same applies to that administrator's API
tokens. Operators and read-only accounts don't have to set it up.

## If you lose your phone {#lost-device}

Without the app, you can't turn off verification from the panel. On the server, as root, run
`panel-api recover-admin --email <your-email> --disable-2fa`. See
[Recover administrator access](./recover-admin-access.mdx).
