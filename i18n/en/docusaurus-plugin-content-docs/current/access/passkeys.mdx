A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. It belongs to the panel's domain,
so a look-alike site can't ask for it: it can't be phished. Use it as the second factor after your
password, or on its own.

:::note
Passkeys work only when you open the panel at its domain name over HTTPS, not at
`https://<server-ip>:8443`: browsers don't offer passkeys to an IP address. Give the panel a domain
first, from **Settings** → **Panel domain and HTTPS**.
:::

## Add a passkey {#add-a-passkey}

1. Open **Security** from your account menu.
2. In the **Passkeys** section, check the suggested **Name** (the browser and system you're on) and
   press **Add a passkey**.
3. Confirm it's you: your password, plus a code, a passkey or a recovery code if you already have a
   second factor.
4. Follow your browser's prompt: your fingerprint, face, device PIN or security key.

The passkey appears in the list. If it's your first second factor, the panel also shows your ten
[recovery codes](./two-step-verification.mdx#recovery-codes): save them. Adding a passkey signs out
your other sessions. You can add up to 20, one per device: a passkey synced by your phone's or
browser's password manager is marked **synced**.

## Sign in with your password and a passkey {#second-factor}

After your email and password, press **Use a passkey** and confirm on your device.

## Sign in with a passkey alone {#passkey-only}

On the sign-in page, press **Sign in with a passkey** and pick your account in the browser's prompt.
No email and no password: the passkey checks that it's you on the device (fingerprint, face or PIN),
which is two factors in one.

## Sign in with passkeys only {#turn-off-password}

Once you have a passkey, you can stop your password from signing in, so a stolen password opens
nothing:

1. In the **Passkeys** section, next to **Sign in with your password**, press **Turn off**.
2. Confirm with a passkey that checks it's you.

From then on, signing in with your password is refused and only your passkeys work. Keep more than
one passkey. To allow the password again, press **Turn on** and confirm with a passkey. An
administrator who resets your password also turns password sign-in back on.

## Remove a passkey {#remove}

1. In the **Passkeys** section, press **Remove** next to the passkey and confirm.
2. Confirm it's you.

The panel signs out your other sessions. You can't remove your last passkey while password sign-in
is off. If it was your last second factor, your recovery codes and API tokens go with it.
