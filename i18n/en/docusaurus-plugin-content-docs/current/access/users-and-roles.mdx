Each account has one of three roles:

| Role | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Full access to the server. |
| **Operator** | Access to assigned sites only. |
| **Read-only** | Can see everything but change nothing. |

For the full breakdown of what each role can do, see [roles and permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Add a user {#add-a-user}

1. Open **Users** and press **Add user**.
2. Fill in **First name** and **Last name** (optional), **Email** and a **Password** with at least 12
   characters.
3. Choose a **Role**.
4. For an **Operator**, choose at least one site under **Assigned sites**.
5. Press **Add user**.

The email address is what the person signs in with, and it must be unique. Send them the password
through a secure channel. They can change it later from **Security**.

## Change a user's role or sites {#change-role-or-sites}

1. In **Users**, open the **Actions** menu on the user's row and choose **Edit user**.
2. Change the **Role** or **Assigned sites**.
3. Press **Save**.

Role, sites and name are saved together. If the panel rejects the change, nothing is saved. Removing
someone's administrator role also revokes their API tokens.

## Disable an account {#disable-an-account}

In **Edit user**, turn on **Account disabled** and press **Save**. The person can't sign in anymore,
their sessions end and their API tokens are revoked. Turn the switch off to re-enable the account.

## Reset another user's password {#reset-a-password}

From the **Actions** menu, choose **Reset password**, enter the new password and press
**Reset password**. That account's sessions and API tokens are closed. To change your own password,
use **Security** instead.

## Delete a user {#delete-a-user}

From the **Actions** menu, choose **Delete** and confirm. The account's sessions, tokens, site
assignments and two-step verification are deleted with it.

## Restrictions {#limits}

- You can't change your own role, or disable or delete your own account.
- The panel always keeps at least one enabled administrator. It won't demote, disable or delete the
  last one.
- An operator must have at least one site. The panel won't save an operator without one.
- Users and tokens can only be managed from a browser session in the panel, never with an API token.

Every change is recorded in the **Audit log**.
