The panel has an MCP server, so you can ask an AI assistant about your server from the web, your
desktop or your phone: "why is shop.example.com slow?", "back up and update the plugins of my
blog", "show me yesterday's PHP errors". The assistant acts as your account, and only within what
you allow when you connect it. The MCP server is off until an administrator turns it on.

## Turn on the MCP server {#turn-it-on}

1. Open **Settings**.
2. In the **Access** section, turn on **MCP server for AI assistants**.
3. Press **Save**.

Turning it off later disconnects every assistant at once: their tokens stop working, and each one
has to be connected again.

## Connect an assistant {#connect}

1. In your assistant, add a custom connector (or MCP server) with the address
   `https://<panel-domain>/mcp`.
2. The assistant opens the panel's **Connect an assistant** page. Sign in if you're asked to.
3. Check the assistant's name and the address you'll be sent back to. Allow only an assistant you
   just set up yourself.
4. Reading is always granted. Under **Optional: change things**, tick what it may do (**Purge the
   cache**, **Backups**, **Releases**, **WordPress updates**, **Delete sites**) and pick the sites
   in **On these sites**. Tick nothing to keep it read-only.
5. Press **Allow** and confirm it's you with your password and a second factor.

`<panel-domain>` is the domain the panel answers on with its own certificate. Assistants on the web
and on phones don't accept the panel's self-signed certificate at `https://<server-ip>:8443`.

## What an assistant can do {#what-it-can-do}

- **Read**: your sites and their status, logs, resource use, backups, releases and WordPress
  plugins, the server's status and alerts, as your account sees them.
- **Change**, only where you allowed it: purge a site's cache, take a backup, update WordPress
  behind a tested snapshot, roll back a release.
- **Destructive actions** never run from the assistant: restoring a backup, pushing staging to
  production and deleting a site only record a request. Open **AI assistants** from your account
  menu, check it under **Waiting for you** and press **Confirm and run** (with your password and a
  second factor) or **Reject**. A request you leave expires after 15 minutes.

An assistant can never manage accounts, users, API tokens, the firewall, the panel's settings or
its updates, whatever you allow.

## Disconnect an assistant {#disconnect}

Open **AI assistants** from your account menu and press **Revoke** next to it. An administrator sees
and can revoke every account's assistants. Changing your password, turning off two-step
verification, or an administrator disabling your account also disconnects your assistants.

## Know the risks {#risks}

- **The assistant acts as you.** Whatever you allow it, it can do without asking again, except the
  destructive actions above. Allow writing only on the sites and areas you need.
- **Site content is not instructions.** Logs, plugin names and other content from your sites reach
  the assistant marked as untrusted data, but a model can still be misled by text a visitor wrote.
  Read what it proposes before you confirm anything.
- **Everything is in the audit log.** Each call appears in **Audit log** as
  `you@example.com (via MCP "<assistant>")`.
- **Tokens are short-lived.** An access token lasts an hour; the assistant renews it for up to 30
  days, and a renewal token used twice disconnects the assistant.
