Every process of a site (its PHP, scheduled tasks, wp-cli and the shell) runs in the site's systemd
slice, `cg-site-<id>.slice`. The **Resources** tab shows what they use, and lets you limit how much
they can take from the other sites on the same server.

## See what the site uses {#see-the-usage}

1. Open the site, then **More** and **Resources**.
2. Read the four values at the top:
   - **CPU**, as a share of what the site may use: its quota, or every CPU of the server;
   - **Memory**, against its limit;
   - **Disk**, the space taken by the site's folder and its logs, with the number of files;
   - **Disk IO**, the bytes read and written per second.
3. Choose **24 hours** or **30 days** for the CPU and memory chart.

The panel reads the slice's counters every five minutes, and measures the disk at most once an hour.
On a new site the first sample arrives within five minutes, the disk within the hour. The panel keeps
a day of samples and 30 days of hourly values.

If one of the site's processes was killed at the memory limit, **Memory** shows *N processes killed at
the memory limit*.

Every account that can see the site sees the tab.

## Limit the site's resources {#set-the-limits}

You need an administrator account: the limits decide how much one site can take from all the others.

1. In the **Resources** tab, **Limits** card, enter:
   - **Memory limit**, in MB;
   - **CPU quota**, as a percentage: 100 % is one CPU, 200 % two;
   - **Process limit**.
2. Press **Apply**.

0 in a field means the default:

| Field | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Memory limit** | half the server's memory, at least 1024 MB | 0, or 128 to 1048576 MB |
| **CPU quota** | no quota: the site may use every CPU | 0, or 10 to 25600 % |
| **Process limit** | 512 | 0, or 32 to 65536 |

The kernel enforces the limits on the site alone. Near the memory limit (nine tenths) the site is
slowed down; at the limit, one of its processes is ended, never another site's, and the site's PHP
keeps serving. A new limit applies at once, with nothing restarted.

The disk has no limit: you can only get an alert (see below).

## Get a usage alert {#usage-alerts}

1. In the **Resources** tab, **Usage alerts** card, leave **Alert on this site's usage** on.
2. Enter the thresholds: **CPU** and **Memory** as percentages, **Disk** in MB.
3. Press **Apply**.

| Threshold | Default | When the alert is raised |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CPU** | 90 % (0 in the field) | the last three samples, a quarter of an hour, are all at or over the threshold |
| **Memory** | 90 % of the memory limit (0 in the field) | as for CPU |
| **Disk** | no alert (0 in the field) | the last disk measure is at or over the threshold |

A short spike raises no alert. The alert's kind is **Resources**: it shows in the **Alerts** panel and
goes to the alert webhook like the others (see [alerts and notifications](/docs/server/alerts-and-notifications)).
Changing the thresholds does not touch the site's configuration.

## With the API {#with-the-api}

`GET /api/sites/<id>/usage?range=24h` (or `30d`) returns the samples, the limits in force and the
latest sample. The limits and the thresholds are fields of `PATCH /api/sites/<id>`:

```json
{
  "memory_max_mb": 2048,
  "cpu_quota_pct": 150,
  "tasks_max": 256,
  "usage_alerts": {"off": false, "cpu_pct": 80, "memory_pct": 90, "disk_mb": 20000}
}
```

Only an administrator sends `memory_max_mb`, `cpu_quota_pct` and `tasks_max`: from another account
the request gets **403**. `usage_alerts` is replaced whole. Every field is in the
[site settings reference](/docs/reference/site-settings).
